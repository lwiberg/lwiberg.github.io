Project

Real Big Machines

Purpose

A YouTube channel focused on fascinating industrial machines

My Role

3D Artist

Scope

Create a 3D model and an average of 2 minutes of animation per video in under a week

Result

4 videos about 4 different machines: Marine Railway, RORO Ship, Potato Harvester, and Oil Rig

Machine 01

Where's the Engine?

The most difficult part of this project was explaining how the cable system worked in a simple and clear way. I simplified the technical drawings into an animated 3D diagram that shows how the cable system moves the boats while the engines remain stationary in the on-site building.

Process

From Reference to Model

I created the 3D model of the marine railway from reference images taken on site. The 3D model highlights the important parts of the system and gives a better understanding of how boats load and unload on the sloped railway while staying perfectly level.

Final Work

Finished Animations

These are 3 short excerpts from the final animation. The full video shows the journey from start to end, as well as lots of live action footage. Watch the full video on YouTube.

Machine 02

RORO Ship

The Tønsberg is one of the largest roll-on/roll-off ships in the world. I adapted an existing model to include the multi-level car decks inside, then animated a reveal that peels the ship open to show how vehicles drive aboard and get stacked deck by deck. Model to final render in under a week.

Process

Look Development

I experimented with different backgrounds, lighting, and materials to find a final look for the animation that my client approved.
