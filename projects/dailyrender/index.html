Daily Render Project

In October 2024, I decided to make a simple looping animation every day from scratch in Blender. I was able to experiment with many new techniques and post a new video... every other day.

This project was a great way to improve my speed and try new things. Every scene required new models, textures, animations, and video editing, but I had to fit the entire process into a 24 hour period. This forced me to simplify my workflow and focus on the most important aspects of each render.
