Frequently asked

FAQ

How did you get into 3D?

I have been editing videos since I was a kid. 3D animation felt like the right fit for my technical mind that also wants to create things. My first paid gigs were making visuals for DJs and VJing at clubs on the weekends while working in engineering during the week.

But then I saw an opportunity to use my 3D skills at work by creating an animation of our product being manufactured. Suddenly, our 4 hour manufacturing training was explained in 60 seconds, and I understood the power of 3D animation for communicating complex ideas.

Do you use AI?

I do not use any generative AI to create my final works. I will not use AI unless specifically requested by a client. I do use AI tools for other tasks, such as research and coding, and I am constantly experimenting with AI tools and agents.

What does a typical project look like?

We will start with an intro call to discuss the project. I provide a same-day quote and contract. Once approved, I execute your brief from start to finish, owning the entire production pipeline. I typically include 1 round of revisions.

What kind of projects are you interested in?

I love projects that combine engineering and 3D animation. I have experience working directly with engineering companies as well as engineering Youtubers/educators. I also love to work with indie teams on VFX for films, and I offer live VJing services for events.

How much do you charge?

Projects vary depending on scope and complexity, but typically range from $1,000 to $10,000. I typically charge on a project basis so you know the total cost upfront, but I can also charge hourly if preferred. I offer discounts for long-term collaborations.
