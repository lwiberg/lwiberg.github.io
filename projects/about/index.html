Hi! I’m Leila. I am a 3D artist, VFX artist, and VJ based in Los Angeles.

Three years ago, I got my first GPU and dove into Blender 3D, spending every evening teaching myself until I left the tech world to pursue my dream. I feel like I haven't worked a day since, because I love what I do. I get especially excited about procedural art and new tools.

One of the hard things about being a freelance artist is that I don't get to work with other artists as much as I would like. I love collaborating with other artists, so if you have a project in mind, please reach out through instagram or email!

3D is my passion. Thank you for supporting.
